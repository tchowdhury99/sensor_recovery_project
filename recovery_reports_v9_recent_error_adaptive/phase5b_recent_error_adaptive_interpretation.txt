PHASE 5B METHOD 2: RECENT-ERROR ADAPTIVE-WEIGHT RECOVERY
==========================================================================================

This is a separate experiment from official Phase 5.

Official Phase 5 remains:
  Original V9 trained hybrid recovery using W10_N3_mean1x detection.

Phase 5B Method 2 tests:
  Recent-error inverse adaptive weighting with freeze-on-detection.

Adaptive formula:
  x_adaptive[k+1] = w_naive*x_naive[k+1] + w_cv*x_cv[k+1] + w_arx*x_arx[k+1]

Recent-error weights:
  E_model = sum absolute prediction error over the recent clean window.
  Inv_model = 1 / (E_model + epsilon).
  w_model = Inv_model / sum(all inverse errors).

Freeze rule:
  When PointDetectionFlag is active, weight updates are frozen.
  This prevents attacked measurements from manipulating the adaptive weighting logic.

Window size: 10
Epsilon: 1e-06

Overall result:
  Files evaluated: 24
  Adaptive better than original by RMSE: 24/24
  Adaptive better than original by MAE: 24/24

Mean RMSE metrics:
  Mean attacked RMSE: 0.017911034813894864
  Mean original V9 recovered RMSE: 0.0015963915477515288
  Mean recent-error adaptive recovered RMSE: 0.00043769141917404187

Mean MAE metrics:
  Mean attacked MAE: 0.007610638525531457
  Mean original V9 recovered MAE: 0.0005646334334951755
  Mean recent-error adaptive recovered MAE: 8.68555044646951e-05

Improvement metrics:
  Mean original improvement over attacked RMSE (%): 91.36055217371855
  Mean adaptive improvement over attacked RMSE (%): 97.59728729660122
  Mean adaptive improvement over original RMSE (%): 78.53937161850124

Interpretation:
  Recent-error adaptive weighting improved every evaluated Roll/Pitch case by RMSE.
  It can be considered a successful experimental extension.
