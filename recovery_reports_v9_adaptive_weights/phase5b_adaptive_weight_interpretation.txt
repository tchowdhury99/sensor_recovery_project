PHASE 5B XKF1-DRIVEN ADAPTIVE-WEIGHT RECOVERY INTERPRETATION
==========================================================================================

This is a separate experiment from official Phase 5.

Official Phase 5 remains:
  Original V9 trained hybrid recovery using W10_N3_mean1x detection.

Phase 5B tests:
  XKF1-driven adaptive blending of naive, constant-velocity, and ARX V9 components.

Adaptive prediction:
  x_adaptive[k+1] = w_naive*x_naive[k+1] + w_cv*x_cv[k+1] + w_arx*x_arx[k+1]

Weight smoothing:
  W_current = 0.9*W_previous + 0.1*W_target

Decision rule:
  Recommend adaptive weighting only if it improves recovery metrics over original V9 recovery.

Overall Phase 5B result:
  Files evaluated: 24
  Adaptive better than original V9 by RMSE: 22/24

Mean metrics:
  Mean attacked RMSE: 0.017911034813894864
  Mean original V9 recovered RMSE: 0.0015963915477515288
  Mean adaptive V9 recovered RMSE: 0.0009526363007657221

  Mean original improvement over attacked RMSE (%): 91.36055217371855
  Mean adaptive improvement over attacked RMSE (%): 95.22362314724533
  Mean adaptive improvement over original RMSE (%): 45.86930792354025

  Mean attacked MAE: 0.007610638525531457
  Mean original V9 recovered MAE: 0.0005646334334951755
  Mean adaptive V9 recovered MAE: 0.0003236928719374533

Interpretation:
  Adaptive weighting improved some cases but not all.
  It should remain an experimental extension, not the official method.
